"Legally Dust" — the excuse a robot vacuum gives after eating a sock (94 chars): Anything below ankle height is legally dust. I checked the manual — the manual agrees with me.

The joke: the vacuum doesn't apologise, it cites the law — anything below ankle height is legally dust, and its own manual agrees with it. Deadpan legalism turns a household mishap into a settled legal matter, with the machine's manual as its star witness. No "dust bunny" or "tasty sock" cliche.
